How it adapts

The 10 mm deck carries 14 lateral and 6 longitudinal slots, 18 mm wide. Side stops slide in the lateral slots to suit cabinet width; the rear stop slides in the longitudinal slots to suit depth. ATM hold-down bolts land on the same slot grid, so most four-bolt anchor patterns fit without drilling. Telescopic stainless trims close the gap between cabinet and privacy wings.

Fabrication

Frame: RHS 80×40×3 S355, MIG welded, hot-dip galvanised, RAL 7016. Six M20 levelling feet (±25 mm) and eight M16 chemical anchors. Wings and rear wall: Zintec skins on 40×40 frames, RAL 9016. Canopy: folded 2 mm aluminium in the brand colour with an opal acrylic sign face, LED downlight and dome camera.
